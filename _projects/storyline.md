# Climate Change Storyline

Based on data from the World Meterological Organization, this chart shows the global mean temperature from 1850—2024. This project considers the history of climate science in relation to the global mean temperature. 

This is also a demo of Knight Lab's Storyline tool by Colleen Nugent McLean.
